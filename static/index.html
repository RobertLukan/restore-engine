Restore progress

Concurrency changes apply within ~1s without restarting the worker. Pause stops new claims; in-flight jobs keep running until they finish or fail. Stop pending cancels queued jobs only. Live restore progress is the PVE restore task (data stream), not guest boot readiness. Live jobs report transferred X of Y (P%) (QEMU block-job); normal jobs use PBS progress/zeroes. Est. wire ≈ virtual × fidx sparsity × compression (live) or (read − zeroes) × compression (normal).
